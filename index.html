About Me

Hello, I am Syahren Maulana Adil Hakim. I am an Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Airlangga, and a graduate of Telkom Malang Vocational School majoring in Software Engineering. I have a strong interest in Android App Development, Web Development, and Digital Marketing. My background in software engineering has given me a solid foundation in programming and problem solving, while my current studies at Universitas Airlangga allow me to deepen my knowledge in system design, data analysis, and modern technologies. During my learning journey, I have experienced various challenges, from adapting to new learning methods at university to collaborating in team projects with diverse backgrounds. These experiences taught me how to manage time, communicate effectively, and be persistent in solving problems. Some of my proudest moments include successfully completing several academic projects, exploring new frameworks, and actively participating in group assignments that strengthened both my technical and soft skills. Looking forward, my goal is to continuously improve my skills in mobile and web development, contribute to impactful digital solutions, and prepare myself for professional opportunities in the tech industry. I also hope to combine my technical expertise with my passion for digital marketing to create innovative projects that can reach and benefit more people.
